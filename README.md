# Sum up 24 November's world news in one image

Program that sums up world news from Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image.

Run `python3 main.py`. It writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB).
Deterministic, standard library only, no network, no randomness.

Stories covered:
1. Hacked data puts Russian death toll in Ukraine near 500,000
2. Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya in drone attack
3. Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after Trump push
4. NATO warns Russia may launch nuclear space weapon next month
5. UN: wars put food aid at risk for 7.3 million more people
6. US named a host of the 2031 Womens World Cup

Sources: Japan Times, SCMP, Manila Times, 24 November 2026.
